## About DDP Atlas

DDP Atlas is a China-origin international freight forwarding and digital logistics platform operated by **SHENZHEN YUJINTONG INTERNATIONAL FREIGHT FORWARDING CO., LTD.**

We help international importers, e-commerce businesses, Amazon sellers, and commercial buyers plan and coordinate shipments from China to global destinations.

Our services include DDP shipping, international air freight, ocean freight, supplier consolidation, customs coordination, and door-to-door logistics solutions.

Alongside freight forwarding services, we develop practical digital shipping resources, logistics calculators, and data-driven freight intelligence tools.

## What We Do

**International Freight Forwarding**

- DDP and door-to-door shipping from China
- International air and ocean freight
- FCL and LCL container shipping
- Supplier consolidation and cargo coordination
- Amazon FBA and e-commerce logistics
- Customs and destination delivery coordination

**Freight Intelligence & Digital Tools**

- International shipping connectivity indicators
- Port throughput and trade data resources
- Freight planning and shipment calculators
- Logistics market research and shipping guides
- Source-based logistics data visualizations
